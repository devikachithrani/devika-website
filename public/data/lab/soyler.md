My research investigates the use of the HAVDI peptide as an alternative to RGD for functionalizing gold nanoparticles in cancer therapy. The performance of HAVDI-functionalized gold nanoparticles will be compared with PEG, PEG linear RGD, and PEG cyclic RGD gold nanoparticles. The objective is to determine whether HAVDI can enhance immune clearance while minimizing toxicity and preserving high tumour-cell-specific internalization.
