Sam and Anthony presented their summer research at the 2025 Canadian Undergraduate Medical Physics Conference. Anthony presented a talk titled "Semi-Automated Quantification of Radiation Induced DNA Double-Strand Breaks for In Vitro Cancer Cell Assays", and Sam presented a talk titled "Differential Uptake of Cyclic RGD-Functionalized Gold Nanoparticles by Prostate Cancer Cells". They did an exceptional job!

Congratulations to Sam who won the People's Choice Presentation Award for the Experimental Methods session of this conference!
