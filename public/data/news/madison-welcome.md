We welcome Madison Blatchford to our lab! Madison completed her MSc at the University of British Columbia Okanagan, where her thesis was the evaluation of spatial and dosimetric accuracy of adaptive radiotherapy on Ethos using polymer gel dosimetry. We are thrilled to have Madison here, and excited to see what she accomplishes!
